Malus Cards

🔥

Fire

Lose 3 to 8 Food

A destructive fire burns part of your food reserves.

🌪

Storm

Lose 3 to 8 Wood

A violent storm damages your supply chains and destroys wood reserves.

🔧

Sabotage

Destroy a random non-capital building

Enemy agents infiltrate your empire and sabotage your infrastructure.

💰

Thieves

Lose 2 to 6 Gold

Thieves steal part of your treasury during the night.

💣

Trapped Building

A random non-capital building is trapped

A hidden trap sabotages one of your buildings, disabling its effects for one turn.

Card System

Every turn, players receive three random card choices:

  • Resource Card → Instantly grants resources
  • Building Card → Allows a free building construction
  • Malus Card → Applies a negative event or penalty

Choosing the right card at the right moment can completely change the future of your empire.
