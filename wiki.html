Game Cards Wiki

Learn every card available in Timeless Empire and build the perfect strategy.

Resource Cards

🌲

Wood Reserve

+10 Wood

A hidden forest reserve is discovered, giving your empire a large supply of wood.

🍖

Abundant Hunt

+10 Food

Your hunters return with exceptional catches.

🪙

Gold Mine

+6 Gold

A new gold deposit is discovered in your territory.

🚚

Merchant Caravan

+5 Wood / +5 Food

A caravan arrives bringing supplies and goods.

Building Cards

🌾

Fast Construction

Build a Farm for free

A team of expert builders quickly constructs a farm.

Bonus: +3 Food per turn

🛡

Barracks Gift

Build Barracks for free

Your military supporters fund the construction.

Bonus: +1 Gold per turn

Malus Cards

🔥

Fire

Lose 3–8 Food

A destructive fire burns your food reserves.

🌪

Storm

Lose 3–8 Wood

A violent storm damages your supply chains.

🔧

Sabotage

Destroy a random building

Enemy agents infiltrate your empire.

💰

Thieves

Lose 2–6 Gold

Thieves steal part of your treasury.

💣

Trapped Building

A building explodes next turn

A hidden trap sabotages your infrastructure.

Card System

Every turn, players must choose between three randomly generated cards:

  • Resource Card → Immediate resources
  • Building Card → Free construction
  • Malus Card → Negative event

Choosing the right card can decide the fate of your empire.
